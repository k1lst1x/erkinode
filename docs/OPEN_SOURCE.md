# Your servers. Your freedom.

Erkinode is a free, open-source SSH and SFTP desktop client.

## Official project principles

- Source code and original SVG logo assets are available under MIT OR Apache-2.0, at your option.
- Local SSH/SFTP does not require an account, activation, licensing server, or subscription.
- No paid unlocks for implemented local features or paid limits on saved hosts.
- Passwords, keys, and terminal contents are not sent to a project service. Connections go directly to your chosen hosts.
- No embedded advertising or telemetry.
- You can build the application yourself using the documented steps and lockfile.
- Beta limits and unimplemented features are documented openly. The 24-connection limit is a current process quota, not a pricing tier.

There is currently no cloud service, commercial plan, or donation system. No invented official domain or third-party payment details are included.
